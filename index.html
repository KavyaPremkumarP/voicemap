Keyboard and voice help
  • Click a place name (or press Enter on it) to hear its details and see it on the map.
  • ↑ / ↓ on a place name moves between places and reads each one. Home / End jump to first / last.
  • / jumps to the search box. R repeats the last message. Esc stops speech.
  • Voice commands: “search for Cubbon Park”, “show hospitals”, “show all”, “read nearby”, “next”, “previous”, “read”, “repeat”, “where am I”, “use my location”, “stop”.
